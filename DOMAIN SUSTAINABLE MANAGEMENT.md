# DOMAIN:SUSTAINABLE MANAGEMENT

### Eco-Agent UI (WasteWise 2.0)



#### 1.CORE PROBLEM REGARDING THE TOPIC

Many major cities like Hyderabad, mumbai and also growing cities like Khammam and Warangal 

have a common problem on waste disposal and is still inconsistent despite initiatives like Swachh Bharat Mission.India generates over 150,000+ tons of municipal solid waste per day.

Segregation at source remains below optimal levels in many urban areas.



a)waste segregation is poor 

b)people dont know how to dispose waste

c)inefficient municipal systems

d)improper incentives for citizens

e)no proper recycling ecosystem 



#### MAIN CAUSE:

no awareness regarding sustainable management

lack of smart feedback

Absence of real-time feedback systems

No measurable sustainability tracking



#### 2.ECO-AGENT UI(WASTE WISE 2.0)

A multi-agent AI-powered sustainable waste management ecosystem that connects citizens, municipalities,

&nbsp;and recyclers through intelligent guidance, and predictive analytics.

It is a digital sustainability infra layer



#### 3.AI AGENTS:

##### 1)Citizen agent

Uploads waste image

Gets instant classification regarding the waste 

Receives eco-score

Earns reward points

Views sustainability dashboard



##### 2)AI Classification Agent

Image::Waste category detection

Carbon impact estimation

Disposal suggestion

Educational explanation



##### 3)Collection Optimization Agent

Predicts waste hotspots

Suggests optimized routes

Reduces fuel consumption

Lowers the municipal operational cost



##### 4)Municipality Agent

Dashboard analytics

Area-based segregation rates

Carbon reduction data

Policy decision insights



##### 5)Recycling Partner Agent

Connects recyclers to sorted waste

Tracks e-waste sources

Enables circular economy

##### 5.Behavioral engineering

Why Behavior Change is the Real Problem

The issue isn’t lack of dustbins.

It’s lack of motivation

No instant feedback

No visible impact

No social pressure

and also due to this even campaigns like Swachh Bharat Mission rely heavily on awareness — but awareness alone does not sustain habits.

##### 

##### 6.carbon Impact Visualization

Instead of saying:

"You segregated waste."

It says:

"You reduced 1.2 kg CO₂ — equivalent to planting 2 trees"

&nbsp;and its surely makes a visible impact and people act when impact becomes tangible



##### 7.Sustainability Metrics Model

Create a Sustainability Score (SS Index):

SS = (Segregation Accuracy × 0.4)+(Participation Frequency × 0.3)+(Carbon Reduction × 0.3)

This gives quantitative evaluation for our user
